# Yardstick by Objective

`pauta-vara-por-objetivo` · Paid media

> Before any metric, decide which number judges this campaign.

## What it does

- Maps each platform objective and optimization goal to the single number that decides the verdict.
- Splits metrics into yardstick (decides), diagnosis (explains) and vanity (ignored).
- Prevents the four classic mix-ups: traffic as awareness, CPM as awareness yardstick, video as awareness, downloads as purchases.
- Includes fixed formulas for hook rate and hold rate and a reading of what to fix.

## The method signature

How this skill thinks and works:

- Two campaigns share a bucket only if they share the yardstick.
- If an objective has no measurable yardstick, it gets no verdict. A judgment with the wrong ruler is worse than none.
- The yardstick is one number; the diagnosis is everything that explains it.
