# Anti-slop checklist

## Specificity
- Does it contain at least one detail only this brand could say (number, place, material, name, process)?
- Could the same sentence appear on a competitor's page? If yes, rewrite.

## Proof
- Every claim has evidence on screen or in the caption, or is removed.
- Numbers come with their source or context.

## Voice
- Matches the brand profile: vocabulary, formality, humor.
- Chilean tuteo; local words only if the brand uses them.
- No stacked superlatives, no "revolutionary", "unique", "best" without proof.

## Structure
- The first line works alone.
- One idea, one action.
- Avoid the patterns "not just X, but Y", three-item lists by reflex, and closing summaries that repeat the body.

## Rhythm
- Vary sentence length; read aloud; cut the first sentence if it only warms up.

## Punctuation and decoration
- Few exclamation marks, emojis and hashtags; no long-dash chains.

## Rewrite examples (patterns)
| Slop | Better |
|---|---|
| "Discover the power of quality furniture" | "A solid-oak table that has held 14 dinners in our test kitchen without a wobble" (only if true) |
| "Follow us for more" | "Next week: how to measure your room before buying a sofa" |
| "Game-changing solution" | State the change: what was slow, what is fast now, by how much |
